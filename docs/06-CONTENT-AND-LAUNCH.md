# 06. Content Checklist and Launch

## What the client must provide

### Business details (goes into Site Settings)
- [ ] Logo as **SVG or transparent PNG**, in two versions: the horizontal logo (header) and the round badge (footer, favicon, social). The JPEG supplied has a light grey background, which will show as a box on dark sections
- [ ] WhatsApp number (digits only, international format)
- [ ] Phone number, email
- [ ] Shop address and a Google Maps link
- [ ] Opening hours
- [ ] Instagram, Facebook, TikTok links
- [ ] Areas they deliver to and typical delivery fee or rule
- [ ] Areas covered by maintenance

### Photos and video
- [ ] 10 to 15 best photos of finished tanks, lit, ideally taken at night
- [ ] 2 to 3 short videos (10 to 15 seconds) of showpiece tanks
- [ ] Photos of every ready-made tank for sale (front view, angle, close-up of the glass and corners)
- [ ] Photos of every fish species in stock (clear and bright)
- [ ] Product photos for food and supplies
- [ ] Photos of the workshop and the team
- [ ] Photos from at least 5 past projects (before, during, after if possible)

### Product information (matches the Sanity fields, see `07-PRODUCT-FIELDS.md`)

**Each tank**
- [ ] Name, photos, short description
- [ ] Length x width x height (cm), volume (litres)
- [ ] Shape, glass type, glass thickness (mm), rimless or framed
- [ ] Freshwater, marine or both; overflow type if any
- [ ] What's included (stand, lid, light, filter, heater, sump, background)
- [ ] Weight if known, lead time (in stock or made to order), warranty
- [ ] **Buy online** (exact price and stock) or **Enquire first** (optional "from" price)

**Each fish**
- [ ] Common name, scientific name, clear photo
- [ ] Water type, care level, temperament, diet
- [ ] Adult size, size you sell at, minimum tank size
- [ ] Water conditions (temperature and pH), compatibility and group size
- [ ] Reef safe (marine fish only)
- [ ] Price and how it is sold (each, pair, group of 6), available now

**Each fish food**
- [ ] Brand, product name, pack size (one product per pack size)
- [ ] Food type, floating or sinking, pellet size
- [ ] Which fish it suits, key selling points
- [ ] Ingredients, protein / fat / fibre / moisture from the pack, feeding guide
- [ ] Price, in stock

**Each accessory** (only if the shop sells them)
- [ ] Category, brand, name, photos
- [ ] "Suitable for" (for example tanks up to 250 litres)
- [ ] Specifications (flow rate, wattage...), what's in the box, warranty
- [ ] Price, in stock

**All products**
- [ ] Which ones to feature on the home page

### Text
- [ ] About the business (story, years in business, what makes them different)
- [ ] The 4 steps of the custom build process in their words
- [ ] Maintenance plans: names, what's included, price or "contact us"
- [ ] 5 to 10 FAQs (delivery, fish health, tank warranty, installation, payment methods, custom tank timelines)
- [ ] Delivery and live-fish policy (what happens if a fish arrives unwell)
- [ ] Warranty on tanks
- [ ] 3 to 6 customer testimonials (with permission to use names)

## Suggested FAQs to start with

1. How long does a custom tank take to build?
2. Do you deliver and install tanks?
3. Which areas do you deliver to?
4. How do I pay for my order?
5. What if a fish arrives unwell?
6. Do you offer a warranty on tanks?
7. What is included in a maintenance visit?
8. Can you build a tank for my restaurant or office?

## Pre-launch test checklist

**Content**
- [ ] No placeholder text or images left
- [ ] Every product has photos, price (where required), and correct kind
- [ ] Opening hours, address and phone numbers are right
- [ ] Policies are written

**WhatsApp**
- [ ] Floating button opens the correct chat
- [ ] Tank inquiry message looks right (product name, link, details)
- [ ] Order message looks right (items, total, method, address)
- [ ] Custom tank and maintenance forms work
- [ ] Tested on iPhone, Android and desktop (WhatsApp Web)

**Site**
- [ ] Tested at 360px, 768px and 1280px widths
- [ ] All links work, no 404s
- [ ] Cart survives a page refresh
- [ ] Out-of-stock items cannot be added to the cart
- [ ] Lighthouse mobile scores: Performance 90+, Accessibility 100, SEO 100
- [ ] Page titles and descriptions set on every page
- [ ] Favicon and share image (Open Graph) set

**Sanity**
- [ ] Live Studio login works (CORS set)
- [ ] The owner has an editor account and has changed something themselves
- [ ] Draft documents are published

## Launch day

1. Point the domain to Vercel and confirm HTTPS works.
2. Submit the sitemap in Google Search Console.
3. Verify or update the Google Business Profile with the website link.
4. Add the website link to Instagram, Facebook, TikTok bios and WhatsApp Business profile.
5. Share the site with 5 friends and ask them to try ordering and inquiring. Watch where they hesitate.

## First 30 days

- Check WhatsApp daily and reply within minutes where possible.
- Add new tanks, fish and projects weekly. Fresh content helps SEO.
- Review Vercel Analytics: which pages bring visitors, which products get clicks.
- Ask happy customers for a Google review and a testimonial.
- Fix whatever customers ask about repeatedly by adding it to the FAQ.
